TURBO CIRCUIT

a tribute to Turbo Esprit (ZX Spectrum, 1986)

Tail the amber suspect car through the grey city grid. Get close before they clock you and they'll bolt — once spotted they're faster, so close in quietly. Get close at speed to make the bust before time runs out. Clip a building and you'll stall; clip traffic — a police car, an ambulance, a van, a bike or an ebike — and you'll lose a little time.
