TURBO CIRCUIT

a tribute to Turbo Esprit (ZX Spectrum, 1986)

Drive the grey city grid and run down the amber suspect car before your fuel or the clock runs out. Get close at speed to make the bust. Clip a building and you'll stall; clip a police car and you'll lose time. Cross a pale fuel pad to refill.
