TURBO CIRCUIT

a tribute to Turbo Esprit (ZX Spectrum, 1986)

Drive the grey city grid and run down the amber suspect car before the clock runs out. Get close at speed to make the bust. Clip a building and you'll stall; clip a police car, an ambulance or a fast motorbike and you'll lose time.

← → / A D change lane — tap it near a junction to turn instead
