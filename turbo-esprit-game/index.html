TURBO CIRCUIT

a tribute to Turbo Esprit (ZX Spectrum, 1986)

Tail the amber suspect car through the walled city. Get close before they clock you and they'll bolt — once spotted they're faster, so close in quietly. Ram them at speed or shoot them to make the bust before time runs out. Clip a building and you'll stall; clip traffic and you'll lose a little time — more for a police car.

← → / A D change lane   Q / E turn left / right at the next corner
