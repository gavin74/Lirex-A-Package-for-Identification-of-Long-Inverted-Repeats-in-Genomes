TURBO CIRCUIT

a tribute to Turbo Esprit (ZX Spectrum, 1986)

Tail the amber suspect car through the walled city. Sit close behind them and their suspicion builds — ease in at their own pace and you can get close quietly, but close in hot and they'll clock you fast. Once spotted they're faster, so watch the SUSPICION gauge. Ram them at speed or shoot them to make the bust before your fuel runs out. Clip a building and you'll stall; clip traffic and you'll burn fuel — more for a police car. Whatever you do, don't hit a dog.
